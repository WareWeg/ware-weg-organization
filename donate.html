Donation questions

Before you give

How do I change or cancel a monthly gift?

Email hello@example.com or call +44 555 0100 240 on weekdays. We will confirm the change in writing within two working days. There is no minimum term and no questions asked.

Can I choose which project my money supports?

Yes. Pick an appeal or programme in step two. Gifts marked “wherever it’s needed most” are the most useful to us, because they let us fill gaps such as emergency repairs that are hard to raise money for.

How does tax relief work?

In countries with a charitable tax-relief scheme, a registered charity can reclaim the basic-rate tax a donor has already paid on their gift. If you tick the box we send a short declaration to confirm you are eligible. You can withdraw it at any time.

Will I get a receipt?

Every donation receives an emailed receipt. Monthly donors also receive an annual statement each spring that lists every gift and any tax relief claimed.
