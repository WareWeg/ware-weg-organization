A small charity with a long memory

Wellspring was started in 2004 by two engineers who kept returning to villages where a donated pump had been broken for years. We exist to make sure the water keeps flowing long after the ribbon is cut.

Mission & values

Everyone, everywhere, with safe water close to home

Our mission is to bring lasting water, sanitation and hygiene to rural communities, working through local organisations and governments so that services outlive our involvement.

Dignity first

We describe people by what they are doing, not what they lack. Communities decide what is built, and they own it.

Built to last

We would rather reach fewer people with water that still runs in ten years than more people for a single season.

Open by default

Functionality data, audits and our mistakes are published. Supporters can see exactly what their money achieved.

Our history

Two decades, one lesson learned many times

  1. 2004

    Founded in Bristol by two water engineers. First 12 pumps repaired in Turkana, Kenya.

  2. 2009

    Ten-year maintenance promise introduced for every new water point.

  3. 2013

    School hygiene programme launches in Ghana and Burkina Faso.

  4. 2019

    Open data map published with the status of every water point.

  5. Today

    1.28 million people reached across nine countries, with 220 staff.
