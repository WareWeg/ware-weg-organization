Volunteer questions

Good to know

Can I volunteer overseas with Wellspring?

No. Our programmes are run by local staff and partner organisations who know the language, the geology and the communities. Sending volunteers would take jobs and time away from them. Skilled professionals can still help remotely through our advisory panel.

Do I need any experience?

Only for the advisory panel and translation roles. For everything else we provide training, and our speakers start by shadowing an experienced volunteer.

Is there a minimum age?

Volunteers need to be 16 or over, and 18 or over for roles involving schools. Younger supporters are welcome at events with a parent or guardian.
