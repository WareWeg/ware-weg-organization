How we work

We don’t drill and leave

Too many rural water points stop working within a few years of the opening ceremony. Ours keep running because the community owns them, local people can fix them and we keep checking.

  • Community-owned from day one

    Each village elects a water committee, chooses the site and sets a small user fee that pays for spare parts.

  • Local mechanics, local parts

    We train and equip area mechanics and stock parts in district towns, so a broken pump is fixed in days, not months.

  • Measured in the open

    Every water point is mapped and checked twice a year. We publish the functionality data, including the ones that failed.
