Wellspring — Free Charity Website Template by uiCookies
https://uicookies.com/

Wellspring is a free website template for international charities and NGOs,
shown as a fictional clean-water and sanitation charity working in nine
countries. It ships six pages: a homepage with impact numbers, a quick-give
card, region tabs, programme cards, a featured story and a "where your money
goes" donut chart; a donation form with one-off and monthly giving, preset
amounts that say what they pay for, tax relief and a dedication option; a
programmes page with progress toward goals; a stories page with a region
filter and a full long-form story; an about page with team, governance, an
annual report link and five years of financials; and a volunteer page with
roles, events and an application form. Deep water teal and jerrycan yellow,
Young Serif headings and Public Sans text. Built on Bootstrap 5.3.8 with
vanilla JavaScript and no jQuery. Charts are drawn in SVG from small data
arrays, every section is visible without JavaScript, and animations respect
prefers-reduced-motion.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  index.html          Homepage: hero, quick give, impact, regions, programmes,
                      story, spending chart, partners, newsletter
  donate.html         Donation form with live summary (front-end demo only)
  programmes.html     Five programmes with costs and progress toward goals
  stories.html        Story list with region filter, plus one full story
  about.html          Values, history, team, governance, annual report,
                      financials chart and table
  volunteer.html      Volunteer roles, fundraising events, application form
  css/vendor/         Bootstrap 5.3.8 (minified, self-hosted)
  css/style.css       Design layer: colours and fonts as custom properties
  js/vendor/          Bootstrap 5.3.8 bundle (includes Popper)
  js/main.js          Vanilla JavaScript, one function per feature
  img/                Public-domain placeholder photos
  CREDITS.txt         Source and licence for every photo

NOTES
  - No jQuery and no other JavaScript libraries. Icons and charts are
    inline SVG.
  - Donation amounts and their impact lines, the spending split, the story
    chart figures and the five-year financials live in arrays at the top
    of js/main.js. Keep the spending list in index.html in step with the
    SPEND array.
  - The donation form takes no payment. It validates and shows a thank-you
    state; connect it to your payment provider before going live. The other
    forms also need a form service or backend to receive submissions.
  - Team and trustee avatars are CSS initials. The annual report link and
    policy links point to "#": replace them with your own files.
  - Replace the placeholder photos in img/ with your own before launch.
    Avoid images that show people in a way they have not agreed to.
